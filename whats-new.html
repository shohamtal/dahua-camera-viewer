What's new in 1.2

Dahua Camera Viewer has been updated.

Fix for NVRs with more than one camera. Older versions looked up the recordings of the previous camera, so camera 2 showed camera 1's video, and some cameras showed no recordings at all. Each camera now shows its own recordings.

Recordings

  • A calendar shows which days have video.
  • Each day has a 24-hour timeline. Click or drag anywhere to play from that time, and zoom in to 1 hour or 10 minutes.
  • Faster playback: 2x, 4x, 8x and Max.
  • ◀ Motion ▶ jumps to the previous or next motion event, if your NVR records motion.
  • Export MP4 saves up to 20 minutes as a file that plays on any phone or computer. The video is copied as is, so there's no quality loss.

Other changes

  • Report a problem (⚑ in the top bar) shows you a report with addresses, serial numbers and names removed, and opens a GitHub issue with it. Nothing is sent unless you submit it.
  • A warning when the NVR's clock is off, for example when daylight saving time isn't set.
  • Playback no longer stops at the end of each hour, and when you jump on the timeline the last picture stays up with a spinner instead of a black screen.
