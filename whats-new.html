What's new in 1.2

Dahua Camera Viewer has been updated.

Important fix for NVRs with more than one camera. Earlier versions searched and played recordings of the previous camera (camera 2 showed camera 1's video, and so on), and some cameras showed "no recordings". Every camera now shows and plays its own recordings.

Recordings, rebuilt

  • Calendar that marks the days that actually have video.
  • One 24-hour timeline per day — click or drag anywhere to play from that moment. Zoom 24h / 1h / 10m.
  • Fast playback: 2×, 4×, 8× and Max.
  • ◀ Motion ▶ jumps between motion events (when the NVR records motion).
  • Export MP4: save up to 20 minutes as an .mp4 that plays on any phone or computer — full quality, no re-encoding.

Also new

  • Report a problem (⚑ in the top bar): shows you a report with addresses, serials and names hidden, and opens a pre-filled GitHub issue — nothing is sent unless you submit it.
  • A warning when the NVR's clock is off (for example, daylight saving time not set).
  • Playback no longer stops at the hour mark, and jumping on the timeline keeps the last picture with a spinner.
